Some Chinese characters are just pictures 🌳

木 mù = tree
林 lín = woods (two trees)
森 sēn = thick with trees (three trees)
森林 sēnlín = forest
日 + 月 = 明 míng: sun + moon = bright
女 + 子 = 好 hǎo: woman + child = good
