In Chinese, almost every feeling lives in your heart ❤️
Which heart are you today? 👇

小心 xiǎoxīn = be careful (small heart)
開心 kāixīn = happy (open heart)
傷心 shāngxīn = sad (hurt heart)
放心 fàngxīn = don't worry (put your heart down)
粗心 cūxīn = careless (rough heart)
