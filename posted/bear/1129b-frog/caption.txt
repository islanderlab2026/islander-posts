In Chinese, someone who has never seen the world is literally "a frog in a well" 🐸

井底之蛙 jǐng dǐ zhī wā = a frog in a well → someone with a narrow view of the world

Send this to the friend who never leaves town 🐼

Pinyin and meaning checked with Taiwan's Ministry of Education dictionary.
